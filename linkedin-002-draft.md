### Cold Open

Sometimes, a throwaway line in a comment living in someone else's world boomerangs right back at you with force.

This is the story of one such comment.

---

### The Comment (verbatim)

So an AI gives me something I don't have, and it is a Pass/Fail class of hard knocks, nose to the gritty bricks of the Vibe Coding Wall, scaling it inch by inch. I can't take the wheel from the AI, I must learn to trust and I must learn to verify.

Does it work? It does, but it is a discipline of discipline, that is for sure. The Kanban board sets me free.

---

### The Reference

Доверяй, но проверяй — *Doveryai, no proveryai.* Trust, but verify.

Reagan borrowed it from the Russians for arms negotiations. Russ watched it happen — Walter Cronkite, Ted Koppel, the whole thing live. He knew exactly what he was doing when he wrote "I must learn to trust and I must learn to verify."

He just didn't expect it to fit this well.

That's the boomerang. Not an accidental quote. A forty-year-old line finally finding the context that deserved it.
