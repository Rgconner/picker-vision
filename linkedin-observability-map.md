# The Observability Quadrant — Article Map

A living map of the LinkedIn body of work. Each article is a dot, placed where it lives on the quadrant. Updated when an article publishes.

---

## The Framework

**X-axis:** Human visibility — what you know vs. what you don't know  
**Y-axis:** System visibility — what your system (Kanban + Bob) knows vs. doesn't know  
**Performance gradient:** Lower-left (least visible, most risk) → Upper-right (fully aligned, maximum performance)

| | **Human knows** | **Human doesn't know** |
|---|---|---|
| **System knows** | **Upper-right: The Arena** — both aligned, full visibility, work flows | **Upper-left: The Signal** — system sees the pattern before you do; partnership earns here |
| **System doesn't know** | **Lower-right: The Fridge** — hidden context; you're carrying it in your head | **Lower-left: The Wall** — unknown unknowns; where projects die |

## Gardner Alignment

The performance gradient (lower-left to upper-right) maps to Howard Gardner's framing of intelligence as a spectrum of capability and context — least capable/visible at lower-left, most capable/visible at upper-right. The system's job is not to maximize productivity. It is to move every thread toward upper-right and to shrink lower-left over time.
