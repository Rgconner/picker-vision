Why the Team Estimate Is Larger
The larger figure is not a claim about wasted time. It is the expected cost of conventional delivery for the same implemented product scope: computer vision, mobile scanning UX, real-time state synchronization, pack-and-verify flow, diagnostics, deployment infrastructure, demo shop assets, and documentation.
The evidence-backed story is simple: about 38.5 hours of direct architect effort produced a system whose delivered scope had already been estimated as the work of a traditional multi-person software team over roughly 9.5 weeks.
