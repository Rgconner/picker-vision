Client Goals & Success Criteria

Moses outlined the requirements for a successful security and AI strategy, emphasizing that AI security cannot exist without robust data security.

  • Holistic Visibility (360-degree view): A single pane of glass that integrates AI identity with data security.
  • Data Landscape Visibility: Understanding where sensitive information (PII, AIP, financial data) resides, its exposure, and current permission levels.
  • Gen AI Agent Oversight: The ability to track what AI agents are doing, where they are moving within the network, which users are employing them, and monitoring for malicious prompts.
  • Policy Enforcement: The ability to enforce granular policies, such as restricting an AI agent's access to specific databases or file shares.
  • Agnostic Integration: The solution must be able to plug into various environments (on-prem or cloud) to accommodate Bunzel's decentralized and acquired company structure.
